Middor

أدوات النظام

Middor is a free and open-source Android application for mirroring apps on your device. With built-in support for horizontal flip and 180° rotation, Middor can be used to: - Display apps on a HUD - Project content onto a car windscreen (e.g. Google Maps navigation) - View mirrored images and videos
