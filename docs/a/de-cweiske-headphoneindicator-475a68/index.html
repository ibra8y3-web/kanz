Headphone indicator

أدوات النظام

Shows a notification icon in the status bar when headphones are plugged into the phone. Shows a different icon when a headset is attached. Both 3.5mm audio jack devices and USB headphones/headsets are supported. The app aims to be a tiny as possible. No ads, no analytics - just its core functionalit
