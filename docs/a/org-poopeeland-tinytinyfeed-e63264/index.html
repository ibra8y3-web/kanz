Tiny Tiny Feed

إنترنت ومتصفحات

A Simple Widget to publish your Tiny Tiny RSS Feeds (https://tt-rss.org) on your home screen. You need to have an account on a Tiny Tiny RSS installation (v1.12 at least) with the APIs enabled in order to use this widget. The widget updates itself every 30 minutes and shows you the last 20 articles
