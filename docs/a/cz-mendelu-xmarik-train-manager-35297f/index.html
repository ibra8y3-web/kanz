hJOP Driver

تعليم وعلوم

With hJOP Driver you become an engine driver and control your engines on the model railroad controlled by hJOP (http://hjop.kmz-brno.cz). hJOP is required for this application to work. The application connects directly to the hJOPserver via WiFi and requests engines from railroad dispatchers.
