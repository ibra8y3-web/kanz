SupIR is an open-source general-purpose IR blaster for Android using the irdb database. Inspired by the now-abandoned AndroidInfraRed , SupIR puts the power of a Universal Remote or a Flipper Zero in your pocket. SupIR requires an Android phone with an IR blaster.
