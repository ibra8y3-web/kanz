ClipVault

أدوات النظام

ClipVault is a deliberately small clipboard manager: one screen with a search bar and your copied entries, and one settings screen. Nothing else. Tap any past entry to put it back on the clipboard - it's marked as the active entry, and selecting it never creates a duplicate or reorders your history.
