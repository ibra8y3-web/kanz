Outspoke

أدوات النظام

Outspoke is a free and open-source speech-to-text keyboard for Android. All transcription happens entirely on your device using the NVIDIA Parakeet ASR model via ONNX Runtime - no audio ever leaves your phone, no account required, and no internet connection is needed after the model is downloaded.
