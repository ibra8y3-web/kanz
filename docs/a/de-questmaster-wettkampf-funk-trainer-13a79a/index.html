Competition Radio Trainer helps firefighters prepare for the Hessische Feuerwehrleistungsübung (HFLÜ). The app supports practice of radio calls, radio conversations and commands used in typical HFLÜ scenarios. Typical training content: - Ausrückemeldung - Eintreffmeldung - 1st and 2nd Lagemeldung -
