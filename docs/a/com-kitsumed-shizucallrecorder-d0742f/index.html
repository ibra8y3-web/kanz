ShizuCallRecorder is the first non-root FOSS call recorder app for Android 11+ . This app main focus are Cellular/VoLTE calls, however, we do attempt to support Wifi-Calling and VoIP when possible . Third-party app support is not guaranteed , they may work provided they use Android Telecom. Features
