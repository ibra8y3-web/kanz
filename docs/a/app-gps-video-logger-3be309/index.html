GPS Video Logger

صوت وفيديو وموسيقى

GPS Video Logger is an ad-free open source lightweight app to record video and geographical position simultaneously. GPS logging and video tracking is done in an optimized manner. The app offers two modes of recording: Video Mode and Timelapse Mode. It also provides a video player to view your video
