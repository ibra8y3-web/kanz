PaperCript

أمان وخصوصية

PaperCript stores your passwords on paper, encrypted . You encrypt a password with your master password, the app turns it into a card with a QR code, and you print an A4 sheet of cut-out cards. To get a password back, you scan its QR and type the master password. Losing the paper costs you nothing:
