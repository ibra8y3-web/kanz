Geological Time Scale

تعليم وعلوم

The App shows the geological time scale of the history of the earth. The time scale shows the units Eonothem, Erathem, Period, Epoch and Stage. It shows the begin of every period and if it is a GSSP. The time scale can be scrolled up and down if it does not fit on the screen. The view can be changed
