Valentin's PowerTools #018 - Volume Percent Calcul

تعليم وعلوم

VPT#018-VPC is an android app that swiftly calculates the mass of alcohol (in grams) contained in a given volume of a beverage (in milliliters) when alcohol content (Vol.-% or v/v) is known. It uses the formula: mass (g) = volume (mL) * Vol-%/100 * specific weight of alcohol (0.793 g/mL) Features: *
