MetroVault

مال وتسوق

MetroVault turns an Android phone into a secure, air-gapped Bitcoin signing device like a cold-storage hardware wallet. It acts as an offline signer for your watch-only wallets (such as Sparrow, BlueWallet, or Electrum) running on an online device. Your private keys are generated and kept on a phone
